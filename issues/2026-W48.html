⚠️ What Affects Your Week

⚠️City offices observe Thanksgiving Day on Thursday, November 26 and Veterans Day (observed) on Friday, November 27. Plan permits, payments and anything else that needs a city employee for Monday through Wednesday.
⚠️Used cooking oil drop-off runs Saturday, November 28 starting at 8 a.m. at 2829 Elysian Fields Avenue. That is the post-Thanksgiving fry oil, not the kitchen drain.
⚠️SNAP application help with the Louisiana Department of Health is at the Main Library on Monday, November 23 from 10:30 a.m. to 2 p.m., with a second session Wednesday, November 25 at Norman Mayer Library.
⚠️Saturday brings the Bayou Classic to the Superdome at 1 p.m. with the Battle of the Bands there Friday night. If you are driving downtown either day, budget extra time around Poydras and the Dome.
