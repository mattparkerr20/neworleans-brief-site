⚠️ What Affects Your Week

⚠️Ferry service between Chalmette and Lower Algiers is suspended for "several days" after a collision involving the ferry, reported Sunday. If that crossing is part of your commute, plan an alternate route for the start of the week.
⚠️Delays and cancellations at Louis Armstrong New Orleans International were still working through the system as of Saturday, following the nationwide FAA ground stop. Check your flight status before heading to the airport.
⚠️Brake tag inspections remain on pause in New Orleans, with state officials saying there are no rules to enforce. Drivers do not need to chase a renewal appointment this week.
