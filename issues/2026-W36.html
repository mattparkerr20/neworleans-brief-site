⚠️ What Affects Your Week

⚠️Katrina remembrance ceremonies continue this week. The 21st anniversary was observed over the weekend, with memorial services and public gatherings throughout the metro area.
⚠️S&WB power complex issues: The Sewerage & Water Board is investigating contractor oversight in its new $300 million power plant. No service disruptions reported yet, but watch for updates if you experience water pressure or outage changes.
⚠️Lincoln Beach on Lake Pontchartrain is on track to reopen by winter 2027. If you're planning a beach day with kids, this East New Orleans landmark will be back on the map soon.
