⭐ This Week at a Glance

GiveNOLA Day

Tuesday, April 28 • 8:00 PM · Citywide

Tuesday is New Orleans' biggest 24-hour giving day—the one time annual nonprofits see a real surge in small-dollar donations. If you've been meaning to support a local cause, this is when your gift counts most.

Meet the Author: Edna Cummings

Wednesday, April 29 • 4:30 PM · National WWII Museum, New Orleans

A rare chance to hear directly from a Six Triple Eight member about serving in a segregated Army during WWII and breaking barriers as a Black woman officer. History that shaped this city, told by someone who lived it.

Ogden After Hours: Aurélien Barnes

Wednesday, April 29, 6–8 p.m. • 6:00 PM · Ogden Museum of Southern Art

New Orleans trumpeter and Preservation Hall regular Aurélien Barnes blends traditional jazz with Mardi Gras Indian funk—the sound of where this city is headed. $15 gets you live music, food, and a shot at Magnolia Ball tickets at a flash-sale price.

An Evening with Wilco

Friday, May 1 • 6:00 PM · Saenger Theatre

The best live music pick of the week. Jeff Tweedy and Wilco at the Saenger—intimate enough to feel close, grand enough to deliver.

The Meters

Saturday, May 2 • 6:00 PM · Saenger Theatre

The city's funkiest institution, playing a sold-out show at the Saenger. This is New Orleans music at its purest—if you somehow have a ticket, do not miss it.
