Saturday, October 31 at City Park's Popp Bandstand, 54 Dreyfous Drive. Browsing local artists outdoors is free, and the stroller terrain is flat, so it works as a morning stop before the afternoon costume chaos.

⚠️ What Affects Your Week

⚠️The boil water advisory for the Little Woods neighborhood in New Orleans East has been canceled by the Sewerage & Water Board, so tap water there is cleared for normal use again. If you stocked up on bottled water last week, you can stand down.
⚠️Signal timing along Poydras Street has been resynced by the city, so downtown drivers should expect a different rhythm of greens than they are used to this week. Officials say additional intersections are next in line.
